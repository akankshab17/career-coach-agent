- The role blends business, technology, and customer empathy, defining product vision, refining strategy, and ensuring releases deliver measurable impact. [1](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/salesforce-apm)
- A strong product manager understands the full product lifecycle and designs within a complex platform that supports integrations and third‑party applications. [1](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/salesforce-apm)
- They grasp enterprise sales motions and how large business customers evaluate, purchase, and expand Salesforce product investments. [1](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/salesforce-apm)
- They use data‑science‑driven prioritization to make informed trade‑offs, balancing user experience with business needs. [1](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/salesforce-apm)
- At this level, they create scalable solutions that drive long‑term customer success. [1](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/salesforce-apm)
