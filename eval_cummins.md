## 1. Company overview + recent news
Cummins was founded in 1919 in Columbus, Indiana and has grown into a global force aiming for smarter, cleaner power. [2](https://www.cummins.com/en-na/company)
It is the leader in global power solutions, having kept the world running for more than 100 years. [2](https://www.cummins.com/en-na/company)
Cummins designs, manufactures, distributes and services engines and related technologies, including fuel systems, controls, air handling, filtration, emission solutions and electrical power generation systems. [3](https://investor.cummins.com/news/detail/233/company-profile-for-cummins-inc)
The company is a $33.7 billion, ~67,400‑employee power‑systems manufacturer headquartered in Columbus, Indiana. [1](https://www.extern.com/post/cummins-internship-guide)
Cummins emphasizes continuous challenge, engagement, meaningful contributions, career growth, and values differences, inviting candidates to make contributions. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)[5](https://hbcu.cummins.jobs)

## 3. What the role really needs
The early‑career software engineer creates software sold as an integral part of Cummins’ products. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
The role involves identifying stakeholder requirements, developing specifications, and designing, implementing, testing, or releasing software that controls Cummins’ products. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
Engineers investigate product software problems, understand causes, recommend actions, own resolution, and document results. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
The position applies and supports improvement of product software development processes and tools, using coding, compiling, testing processes and tools such as code editors, integration tools, static analysis tools, compilers, and hardware‑in‑the‑loop test tools. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
Engineers obtain input, collaborate with product and software development teams, and deliver verified software features, components, and builds. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
The role uses systems knowledge to decide on software requirements, architecture, design, and test, impacting quality and performance of builds and product lines. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
Engineers own problem resolution for moderately complex components, products, systems, subsystems, or services, with complexity increasing as experience grows. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
The position is responsible for interacting and collaborating with cross‑functional teams. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
The job is located in Wuhan, China. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)

## 4. How to prepare
1. Practice answering behavioral questions on video to be ready for the HireVue recorded round. [1](https://www.extern.com/post/cummins-internship-guide)
2. Review the three‑round interview structure and be prepared to receive honest feedback after each answer. [1](https://www.extern.com/post/cummins-internship-guide)
3. Familiarize yourself with stakeholder requirement gathering and specification development as described for the role. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
4. Brush up on coding, compiling, and testing tools such as code editors, static analysis, and hardware‑in‑the‑loop test tools. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
5. Study problem‑resolution techniques for software issues, including root‑cause analysis and documentation. [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)

## 5. Questions to ask the interviewer
1. Can you describe how new graduates collaborate with product and software development teams to deliver verified software features? [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
2. What opportunities exist for early‑career engineers to take ownership of moderately complex problem resolution and grow technical complexity over time? [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
3. How does Cummins support continuous challenge, engagement, and career growth for engineers working on software that controls product performance? [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
4. Could you share how the team utilizes tools like static analysis and hardware‑in‑the‑loop testing in daily development cycles? [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
5. What is the typical career path for a software engineer starting in Wuhan, and how does the location influence project assignments? [4](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/cummins/2027-early-career-electronic-systems-engineer-software-0b77ba)
