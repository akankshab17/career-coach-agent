## 1. Company overview + recent news
Perplexity, founded in 2021 and based in San Francisco, aims to make search engines without AI a thing of the past. [1](https://www.getbridged.co/company-reviews/perplexity)[3](https://www.forbes.com/companies/perplexity)
The startup was co‑founded in 2022 by Aravind Srinivas, Johnny Ho, Denis Yarats and Andy Konwinski and uses a medley of AI models to answer a wide range of questions. [3](https://www.forbes.com/companies/perplexity)
Perplexity will launch a free agentic shopping product for U.S. users next week, which lets users make seamless purchases directly from answers and is powered by a partnership with PayPal. [4](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/19/perplexity-ai-online-shopping-paypal.html)
The company ships features from idea to production in days, not quarters, and averages 11 days from application to offer across all roles. [5](https://www.extern.com/post/perplexity-internship-guide)

## 4. How to prepare
1. Practice writing clean, efficient code and reviewing core computer‑science concepts. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
2. Work on critiquing AI‑generated outputs and articulating improvements. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
3. Solve coding challenges that require walking through solutions and optimizing algorithmic time complexity. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
4. Debug code snippets and propose more robust implementations. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
5. Design data pipelines that gracefully handle edge cases. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
6. Evaluate trade‑offs of different data structures in realistic contexts. [2](https://dataford.io/interview-guides/perplexity-ai/ai-engineer)
